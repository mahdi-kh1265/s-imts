# Bundled Open / Preprint Papers

The `open_access/` folder contains the papers/preprints gathered for direct study. These are included because an open/preprint or public-government copy was located during the research pass. Publisher-only or explicitly redistribution-restricted files were not copied; see `READING_LIST.md` instead.

1. McCarron 2008 - Rb MTS fundamentals / FWM
2. Preuschoff - MTS modulation-parameter optimization
3. Innes - detailed MTS density-matrix theory
4. Guan - velocity-comb multi-frequency MTS
5. Khalutornykh - recent Li D1 MTS parameter/polarization study
6. Schine - high-modulation-depth FM spectroscopy / 1f and 2f
7. Trinh - modulation-transfer protocol in Rydberg RF sensing
8. Branco - optimization of Rydberg modulation-transfer protocol
9. Miao - dual-optical-transition / dual-wavelength MTS
10. Khan - neighboring/polarization effects in Rb MTS
11. NIST 1984 - classic government report on MTS for stabilizing lasers
12. Kedar - synthetic FM triplet / engineered modulation
13. Thomas - noise-optimal resonant FM spectroscopy
14. Eguchi - 2026 synthetic FM triplet for THz stabilization
15. Mihm - simultaneous MTS with different modulation frequencies
16. Perez Galvan - two-color MTS
